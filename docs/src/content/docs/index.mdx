A narrated short takes a dozen tools to make well: a voice, word timing,
motion graphics that land on a spoken word, captions, a mix that sits under the
voice, and a check that nothing flashes or stalls. Done by hand each time, the
steps drift, and the second video looks nothing like the first.

Film adds **narrated short films** to Keelson. You agree a storyboard, the
**production line** renders the cut from a manifest, a **cut gate** stops it
before anyone sees it, and a gated step stages it for the web. Agents on any
provider, Copilot included, drive it through the rib's tools and workflows, and
read **the method** (how a good short is planned, drawn, voiced, mixed and
reviewed) through the `film_guide` tool.

Nothing needs a cloud account to start. `film_init` writes a sample series with
one short that renders with the machine's own voice and a procedural music bed.
Configure a voice service when the draft is right, and cut again.

Film is a [Keelson](https://keelsonrib.ai/docs/) rib: it installs into the
harness you already run and uses what the harness owns (workflows, approval
gates, provider routing and the MCP tool registry). Everything here is what Film
adds.
