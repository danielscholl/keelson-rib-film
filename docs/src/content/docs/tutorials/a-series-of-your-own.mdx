**The problem.** The sample looks like the sample. You want a run of short
videos on your own subject that look and sound like they belong together,
without restyling each one.

**What you will do.** Start a series with a look, a voice and a bed, have the
agent plan a piece through the method, cut it, and set the gates so the reviewer
answers future cuts.

**You need.** The library from [your first film](../your-first-film/). A voice
service is optional; the system voice works throughout.

## 1. Start the series

Make a folder beside `first-steps` and give it a `series.yaml`:

```yaml
title: Field notes
prefix: ep
theme:
  look: dark
  colors: { accent: "#4fb39a" }
  brand: FIELD NOTES
gates:
  storyboard: human
  cut: { human_first: 2 }
publish:
  dir: published/{series}/ep-{fig}
```

`human_first: 2` means a person answers the cut gate until two pieces in this
series are published, and the reviewer after that.

If a voice service is configured, add a voice:

```yaml
voice: elevenlabs:21m00Tcm4TlvDq8ikWAM
```

[Pick a voice](../../guides/voices/) has the other forms.

## 2. Make its bed

A series sounds like itself when its bed does. From the library root, with
`film` standing for the command `film_doctor` prints:

```bash
film loop assets/beds/field-notes.wav --len 45 --bpm 88 --chords Am7,Fmaj7,Cmaj7,G7 --no-arp
```

Listen to it. Change the chords or tempo and run it again until it fits;
`field-notes.prompt.txt` keeps the recipe.

## 3. Plan a piece with the agent

In chat, give the agent a subject and an audience:

```text
> plan a 30-second field-notes episode explaining why a manifest beats a
> timeline editor, for engineers who've never made a video
```

An agent with the Film rib reads `film_guide` before it plans. Expect it to
work through the [method](../../guides/method/): a brief, two or three angles
for you to pick from, then a storyboard with every line, what each board shows
and on which word, and the sound. The storyboard gate is `human`, so it waits
for you to approve before voicing anything.

Once you approve, it writes `field-notes/ep-01-manifest/short.yaml`, a board
page against the `--film-*` variables, and a `beds` entry pointing at
`assets/beds/field-notes.wav`.

## 4. Cut it and read the evidence

```text
> cut field-notes/ep-01-manifest
```

The cut comes out in the dark look with your accent: captions, cards and the
board all draw from the series theme, and the bed sits under the narration. If
something is wrong, say so at the gate, or edit the manifest, and cut again. `film_status` shows who
answers this series' next cut gate: `human` for now.

## 5. Publish, and let the reviewer in

Approve the cut, then publish it:

```text
> publish field-notes/ep-01-manifest
```

`film-publish` stops for you (the publish gate is always a person), then writes
the web mp4, poster, thumbnail and captions under `published/field-notes/ep-01/`.

After the second piece is published, `film_status` reports the series' cut gate
as `reviewer`. From then on the reviewer reads each cut's evidence first. A
cut it approves goes through; anything else comes to you with its numbered
changes attached.

## What you learned

The series carries the identity, so a piece only has to carry its idea. The
gates start with you and move to the reviewer once the series has shown it cuts
well, and publishing always comes back to you.
